# SSAC-2027
Repository for research papers submitted to the 2027 MIT Sloan Sports Analytics Conference.

Three abstracts were submitted:
1. Language Under Load: Forecasting NBA Injury Risk from Post-Game Interviews (basketball track: injury prediction modelling from language)
2. Repeated Impact, Changing Language: Psycholinguistic Effects of Cumulative Football Exposure (football track: concussion exposure quantification from language)
3. Performance Under Pressure: Measuring Player-Specific Turnover Resilience in the NBA (basketball track: modelling pressure resillience from NBA play-by-play data)
